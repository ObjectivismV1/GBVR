Ghostbusters VR (V4) is a free fan-made mod that lets you play Ghostbusters: The Video Game Remastered in VR. You walk around, aim the proton pack with your own hand, and catch ghosts with real motion. It works with a Quest (through Virtual Desktop, Air Link or Steam Link), Index, Vive and other SteamVR headsets. It supports the Steam version and the Epic Games Store version of the game.

Basic controls (Quest / Touch controllers, right-handed):

Move and look

Walk: left stick. Sprint: click the left stick.

Turn: flick the right stick left or right.

Jump: B. Use / open / pick up: A.

Crouch: duck down in real life.

Fight

Fire the proton gun: right trigger. Alternate fire (Boson Dart): left trigger.

Two-hand aim: put your left hand next to the gun and hold the left grip.

Catch a ghost: click the right stick. Squeeze the left trigger while holding it to slam.

Throw the ghost trap: press X, or take the trap from your left hip and throw it.

Gear

Switch weapon: hold A, push the right stick toward a weapon, then let go.

PKE meter: tap Y to take it out, tap again to put it away. With it out, the right trigger scans.

Vent the pack: put a hand over your shoulder and squeeze a grip.

Holster the gun: put your right hand over your right shoulder and squeeze trigger and grip together.

Menu and settings

Menu: hold your left hand beside your left ear and squeeze the left grip. Point with your right hand and use the trigger to click.

VR Settings (button at the bottom of the menu): turning, sprint, left-handed, seated, comfort options and the view.

Keyboard: F4 headset view on/off, F9 recenter, F12 menu.

Install: unzip, double-click INSTALL.cmd, start SteamVR, then start the game from your launcher.

On the Epic version: the installer looks for it automatically, but I haven’t tried it on a real Epic copy
