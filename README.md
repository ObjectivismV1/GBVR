Ghostbusters VR is a free fan-made mod that lets you play Ghostbusters: The Video Game Remastered in VR. You walk around, aim the proton pack with your own hand, and catch ghosts with real motion. It works with a Quest (through Virtual Desktop, Air Link or Steam Link), Index, Vive and other SteamVR headsets. You need the Steam version or Epic version of the game.

Basic controls:

Walk: left stick. Sprint: click the left stick.
Turn: flick the right stick left or right.
Jump: B. Use / pick up: A.
Fire the gun: right trigger. Alternate fire: left trigger.
Catch a ghost: click the right stick. Throw the trap: X.
Switch weapon: hold A, push the right stick toward a weapon, then let go.
PKE meter: Y.
Menu: hold your left hand beside your left ear and squeeze the left grip.
